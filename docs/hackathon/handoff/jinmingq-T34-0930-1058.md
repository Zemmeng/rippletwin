## 3. For the lead
- This note may not change the page, styles or front-end JS; the existing page's micro-animation was not replaced. SUMO data can only be shown once a new front end connects to `/frame` or the full chunks.
- The safety and congestion metrics come from the same native run; a job fails on any collision, teleport, emergency braking, gridlock or failure to clear, and no incomplete comparison is published.
- The results do not force "the optimisation is always better": the default detour shortens mean bus travel time by 118.48 seconds but lengthens mean car travel time by 1.77 seconds; the maximum queue on the detour section rises from 11.6 to 67.6 metres. After the footpath is closed, the cumulative number of people using the opposite side goes from 90 → 222, and mean pedestrian travel time increases by 21.42 seconds.
- For now this is a limited-scenario backend: cars, buses and pedestrians; no bicycles, trams, weather or understanding of VMS text. The detour compliance share is an input assumption. The model and the defaults are documented in the README.
- It listens only on the loopback address and by default is not open to arbitrary browser origins; `--allow-origin` must be set exactly. A production service needs authentication, resource management and a host for native processes; it cannot be deployed directly as Cloudflare static assets or a Worker.
- Real response samples are provided in `tools/sumo/examples/` for integration; the samples must not be used as a "real simulation" fallback when a run fails.
